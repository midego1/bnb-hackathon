The problem

  • Scooter shops in Thailand usually keep the tourist's passport (or a stack of cash) as the deposit.
  • At return the shop alone decides what each scratch costs. Renters fear bogus damage claims.
  • Shops face real damage with no proof: a few chat photos, if any.
  • Nobody neutral in between, and nobody goes to court over a few thousand baht.
Developer Track · CoinMarketCap x Atlas1 / 10
Solution

The deposit sits in a smart contract made for this one rental. Neither side can move it alone.

1

The shop offers the scooter

Rent, deposit and period in baht. Agreement and guided pickup photos stay off-chain; only their SHA-256 fingerprints go on-chain.

2

The renter funds the escrow

Deposit + month 1 go into the rental's own contract instead of a passport at the counter. Rent is paid out to the shop month by month.

3

Return and settle in minutes

An AI inspection drafts the split. One side proposes, the other accepts, the money moves at once. Disagree? The dispute desk rules.

Timeouts protect the renter: owner silent for 7 days, or the desk misses its 14-day deadline → full deposit back. Every amount is shown in baht first.

→
One party

✓ Agree

KeyEscrow · docs/ARCHITECTURE.md4 / 10
BNB-native

Built on BNB Chain, not just deployed there

One escrow clone per rental

EscrowFactory deploys a 45-byte EIP-1167 proxy of RentalEscrow per rental. Funds isolated per rental, no admin key, not upgradeable. On-chain indexes by owner, renter and desk: no backend database.

ERC-8004 agent #2921

The KeyEscrow Inspector is registered in the ERC-8004 Identity Registry on BSC testnet (BNB Agent SDK). Its id is in every report hash; it speaks A2A and MCP (17 tools).

Evidence on BNB Greenfield

Agreement and photos off-chain, SHA-256 on-chain. Greenfield store (delegated upload) when configured; the Workers deploy uses R2 or hashes in the browser.

opBNB-ready

Plain Solidity, no BSC-specific opcodes. Deploy guard accepts opBNB testnet (5611). Monthly rent releases are cheap on an L2. Not deployed on opBNB yet.

BSC testnet (chain 97) · mainnets refused by the deploy scripts5 / 10
AI inspection + dispute desk

The AI drafts. People decide.

  • Structured result: refund, claim, reasons, confidence. Bounded by the deposit.
  • Recalls each scooter's earlier inspections (Living Brain), so old damage is not charged twice.
  • A party submits it with their own wallet; the other accepts or disputes.
  • Dispute desk: a queue with photos, proposals and the AI report; a hard 14-day deadline.

On the live site today the deterministic demo inspector answers (no model key set); with a key, a vision LLM returns the same JSON.
